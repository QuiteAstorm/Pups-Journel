**November 4, 2026 — founder's planning handoff; not adopted law or bylaws**

## Structure and legal status
One intended Virginia nonprofit, Happy Furrbabbies, centered on Furrever Dog Sanctuaries. Dogs reside at sanctuaries, including lifelong residents and dogs available for adoption. Rescue/adoption, Helping Paws, and community work are integrated. Name reserved with Virginia SCC; nonprofit incorporation and IRS 501(c)(3) recognition remain incomplete.

Research Virginia incorporation, charitable solicitation, animal shelter/rescue/kennel and local facility rules, veterinary standards, federal/state filings and public disclosures.

## Financial controls
Maintain donation purposes and restrictions, spending records, invoices, receipts, payment approvals, donor acknowledgments, retention, board oversight and conflict-of-interest procedures. Distinguish legal obligations from chosen safeguards.

## Helping Paws — firm founder rule
No money directly to applicants: no cash, personal checks, transfers, gift cards or reimbursements. Verify requests and providers, pay veterinarians/pharmacies/suppliers/services directly, deliver goods or arrange pickup, and record approvals, payments and fulfillment. This is an internal policy, not a general government rule.

Eligible assistance: veterinary care and medications, dog food, beds/blankets/grooming, toys/puzzles/enrichment, walkers, supervised play and companionship. My Dog Needs a Play Buddy is dedicated to Jasper (Scruffybutt). Develop volunteer screening, safety and owner permission procedures.

## Outside charities
Verified outside charities may show multiple direct donation methods. Money goes directly to those charities, not Happy Furrbabbies. Verify identities and destinations, distinguish independent charities from own fundraising and verify tax status before promising deductions.
