# Happy Furrbabbies — Project Decisions & Coordination Notes

**Owner-provided handoff: November 3, 2026**

## Mission
“Happy Furrbabbies does not belong to them, it belongs to the dogs.” Future leaders are stewards of a compassionate dog-centered mission.

## Official working program names
- **Happy Furrbabbies:** main community and umbrella organization.
- **Helping Paws:** assistance and support for dogs and their families.
- **Furrever Dog Sanctuaries:** lifelong sanctuary care.
- **Furrever Dog Rescue & Adoption:** dog rescue and adoption.

Dogs remain the focus without categorically excluding other animals.

## Helping Paws
“My Dog Needs a Play Buddy” connects dogs with playmates, companionship, exercise, walkers, and enrichment. Require screening, compatibility, safety, and owner permission. Financial support goes to approved service providers directly, never applicants. Jasper (Scruffybutt) receives a dedication; reserve his real digging photo for that dedication.

## Sanctuary governance and continuity
Proposals: permanent founder board seat and protections, independent directors, daily operational managers, $1,200/month compensation cap for person in charge, donation ledger (donor, recipient, amount, status), contingency for unspent sanctuary-designated funds if operations cease, and compassionate mission-aligned successors without a veterinary credential requirement. Preserve the name and mission. **These are proposals, not legally adopted bylaws.** Legal review, donor restrictions, and applicable law govern implementation.

## Design and accessibility
Readable bold dark text, high contrast, avoid purple as primary interface color, accessible controls, reduced motion, compact dog cards, simple menus, horizontal carousels, tap-outside-to-close behavior, and mobile-first layouts.

## Community Feed
Compact flip cards; comments openly on back; visually distinguish original comments and replies; small avatars by replies; compact treat controls and smaller comment buttons. **Open bug:** front-card content scrolls underneath the treats section; not verified fixed.

## Coordination and status rules
Pup's Journal records coding explanations, decisions, planning, merged PRs, unresolved issues. Chat decisions are **not** automatically code changes, merged PRs, or deployed features. Cross-check decisions before changing another program; avoid duplicating or contradicting plans; verify merges and live deployment; never mark unverified features complete.

**Goal:** one connected dog-centered organization with coordinated programs and reliable records.
