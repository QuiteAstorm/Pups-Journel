# Happy Furrbabbies — Public Mission & Governance Overview
**November 3, 2026 | Planning-stage summary, not adopted bylaws**

Happy Furrbabbies is planning a canine-centered Virginia nonprofit with the guiding principle:

> “Happy Furrbabbies does not belong to them, it belongs to the dogs.”

## Planned programs
- **Helping Paws:** dog companionship, play buddies, volunteer support, and hardship assistance, including **My Dog Needs a Play Buddy**, dedicated to Jasper (“Scruffybutt”).
- **Furrever Dog Sanctuaries:** humane, sustainable lifelong care for dogs needing permanent sanctuary.
- **Furrever Dog Rescue & Adoption:** rescue, rehabilitation, and adoption.

## Proposed accountability
An independent board would oversee financial stewardship, compliance, and strategy, while trained sanctuary managers would direct everyday care. Urgent veterinary treatment would never be delayed for administrative approval. The organization intends to respect donor restrictions and provide transparent financial reporting.

**Status:** These are proposals while incorporation and bylaws are being developed. The website's donation ledger is fictional; it does not process real donations.

Private founder compensation, benefit planning, director appointment powers, and legal drafts are intentionally **not published here**.
